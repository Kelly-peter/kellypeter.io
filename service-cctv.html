  • Recording setup and system testing
  • Remote viewing and maintenance guidance

Benefits

  • Improved visibility in critical areas
  • Reduced risk of theft, intrusion, or incidents
  • Faster response and better investigations

Process

A straightforward setup flow from site review to activation.

1. Assess the site

I review key entrances, blind spots, lighting conditions, and security priorities.

2. Recommend the system

We select the right cameras, recording format, and layout for the property and use case.

3. Install and test

The system is mounted, connected, and checked for coverage, clarity, and recording quality.
